## 2. Interactive Journey steps
Each chapter now includes:
1.  **Overview:** Real-life connections and learning goals.
2.  **Animation:** Visual concept explanation (Lottie/Rive).
3.  **Video:** Teacher-led expert video session.
4.  **Multi-Style Learn:** Choice of Simple, Story, or Deep Dive text.
5.  **Interactive Activity:** Matching or Tap-to-Reveal challenges.
6.  **Practice:** Concept-specific exercises with instant feedback.
7.  **Quiz:** Comprehensive chapter assessment.
8.  **Revision:** Flashcards and memory mnemonics.
9.  **AI Tutor:** Contextual doubt-clearing with Gurukul AI.
10. **Finish:** Reward summary and progress sync.
