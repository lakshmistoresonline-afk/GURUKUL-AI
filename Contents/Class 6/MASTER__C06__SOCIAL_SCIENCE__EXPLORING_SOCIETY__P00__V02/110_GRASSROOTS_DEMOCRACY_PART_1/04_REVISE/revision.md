# Revision — Grassroots Democracy – Part 1

Before we move on ...
	
Æ
No country can run without governance and government.
	
Æ
A modern government has three organs — legislative, 
executive and judiciary — which need to work together.
	
Æ
The Indian government functions at three levels — Centre 
or national, State and local.
	
Æ
Democracy is the overall framework for this system. It 
functions through elected representatives, both at the State 
and the national levels.
