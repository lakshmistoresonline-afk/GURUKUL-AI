# Revision — Oceans and Continents

Before we move on …
	
Æ
The Earth’s surface has vast water bodies called ‘oceans’ 
and large landmasses called ‘continents’. Oceans are 
interconnected. Continents may be counted in various 
ways; the most common count is seven.
	
Æ
The Northern Hemisphere has more land than the 
Southern Hemisphere.
