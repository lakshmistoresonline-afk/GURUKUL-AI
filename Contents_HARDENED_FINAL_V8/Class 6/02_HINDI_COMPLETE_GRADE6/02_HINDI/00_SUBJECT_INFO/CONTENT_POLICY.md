# Content Preservation Policy

1. Preserve source text and source page provenance.
2. Do not silently omit activities, exercises, poems, stories, notes, questions, glossaries, or references.
3. Structural transformations may normalize filenames, folders, whitespace, and metadata.
4. Source PDFs remain authoritative for visual/layout fidelity.
5. OCR/text extraction is stored for data access and search; where extraction is imperfect, the original PDF remains the ground truth.
