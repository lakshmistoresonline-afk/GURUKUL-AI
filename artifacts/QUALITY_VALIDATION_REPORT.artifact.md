# Quality Validation Standards - Phase 56

## Pedagogical Framework
All AI-generated content follows the **Gurukul AI Pedagogical Standard**, focusing on:
1.  **Age-Appropriateness**: Language complexity tuned for 10-12 year olds.
2.  **Socratic Method**: Every lesson includes at least 5 inquiry-based prompts to stimulate critical thinking.
3.  **Culturally Relevant Stories**: Narrative hooks use local contexts (Indian names, festivals, and common scenarios) to increase engagement.
4.  **Bloom's Taxonomy Alignment**: Content spans from *Remember* (Flashcards) to *Apply/Create* (Activities).

## Component Standards
- **Quizzes**: Minimum 10 questions per chapter, including HOTS (Higher Order Thinking Skills).
- **Explanations**: Must include a "Mistake Prevention" section (Common Misconceptions).
- **Visuals**: AI-generated illustration prompts must be optimized for stable diffusion/DALL-E consistency.

## Validation Status
- **Math 5**: Validated for numerical accuracy and logical progression.
- **Science 6**: Validated for scientific terminology and safety in hands-on activities.
